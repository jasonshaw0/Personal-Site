I've started enough projects over the years that adding a blog to the list probably isn't the most sensible decision.

Still, I've wanted somewhere to write about the things I'm working on without having to turn everything into a `README`. 

Some of my projects work out. Others don't. Usually, there's something interesting to take away either way. That's more or less what this space is for.

I'll write about whatever I happen to be building, learning, or thinking about. Some posts will be technical, others probably won't.

Whether I actually keep up with it remains to be seen.

At least the website works.
